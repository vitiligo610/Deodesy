# Welcome to Deodesy

Deodesy is a powerful and comprehensive C#/.NET library for performing geodesic calculations. The name _Deodesy_ is a portmanteau of _.NET_ and _Geodesy_, reflecting its mission to bring robust and accurate geographical computations to the .NET ecosystem.

It is a faithful port of [Chris Veness's renowned JavaScript Geodesy Functions](https://github.com/chrisveness/geodesy), maintaining high precision while providing a first-class developer experience for .NET users.

## What is Geodesy?

Geodesy is the scientific discipline that deals with the measurement and representation of the Earth. It involves understanding the Earth's gravitational field, its geodynamic phenomena (polar motion, Earth tides, crustal motion), and its three-dimensional time-varying distribution of mass.

## Features

Deodesy provides a wide range of functionalities, including:

*   **Geodesic Calculations:** Perform precise calculations for distances, bearings, and other parameters across both spherical and ellipsoidal Earth models. This includes both trigonometry-based and vector-based approaches for maximum flexibility and accuracy.
*   **Ellipsoidal Earth Models:** Support for various ellipsoidal-earth coordinate systems, encompassing both historical datums and modern terrestrial reference frames (TRFs).
*   **Mapping Functions:** Tools for working with popular mapping systems such as UTM/MGRS (Universal Transverse Mercator / Military Grid Reference System) and UK OS Grid References.
